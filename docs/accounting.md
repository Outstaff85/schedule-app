# Accounting and payroll

Hours and pay calculation, the Accounting tab, supplier commission, client billing, the hours-import approval queue, and the exports. The pay functions are `src/lib/payroll.ts`; the Accounting tab's figures `src/manager/accounting.ts`, its screen `src/manager/Accounting.tsx`; the data and the server calls stay in `schedule-manager.html`.

**Rule:** all hours and pay go through the functions in "Canonical calculation" below. Never add a second hours or pay calculation.

## The punch (input data)

A punch is one IN or OUT:
`{id, workerId, workerName, type:'in'|'out', time:'HH:MM', date:'dd/MM/yyyy', ts:ISO, clientId, site, dist, gpsAccuracy, gpsSuspicious, gpsWarnings, notes, manual, editedAt, editedBy}`.
In Firestore it is a document `tenants/{t}/punches/{id}` with `ts` (Timestamp), `day` (`yyyy-MM-dd`) and `archived` added. Imported hours have ids `imp-<batch>-<row>-in|out`.

The client keeps the punch log in `localStorage['punchLog:' + firm]` (`getPunchLog()` / `savePunchLog()`). How it is loaded and kept in sync: [admin.md](admin.md) → Punch log data.

## Canonical calculation

### Payroll module (`src/lib/payroll.ts` → `payroll.js`)

- **`src/lib/payroll.ts` is the one copy** of the functions below (strict TypeScript): `PAY_RULES`, `groupPunchesIntoShifts`, `analyzeDayPunches`, `israeliDayPay`, `effectiveRates`, `shiftPay`, `shiftPayFor`, `isDailyPay`, `dailyRate`, `wOwnRate`, `travelPay`, `salaryPeriodKey`, `dayOverride`, `localYMD`, `roundMins` (stage 2 of [react-migration-plan.md](react-migration-plan.md)). A new page imports it. Two signatures differ from the old page globals: `effectiveRates`, `shiftPay` and `shiftPayFor` take the page's `clients` list as their last argument, and `analyzeDayPunches(entries, date, opts?)` does the manager's full check only with `opts.tr` (below).
- **The old page** (the manager app) loads it as `payroll.js` (`<script src="payroll.js">` in the head, before the page script): a plain script that defines the global `Payroll`, generated from `payroll.ts` by `node scripts/build-globals.mjs` (`npm run build:payroll`) and committed; `npm run build` fails while it is stale (`--check`). Never edit `payroll.js` by hand. The page keeps one-line wrappers with the old names and signatures (`function shiftPay(hours, w, isSat, clientId){ return Payroll.shiftPay(hours, w, isSat, clientId, clients); }` …: `analyzeDayPunches` with `{tr}`, `groupPunchesIntoShifts`, `effectiveRates`, `shiftPay`, `shiftPayFor`, `isDailyPay`, `dailyRate`, `travelPay`, `salaryPeriodKey`, `dayOverride`, `localYMD`, `wOwnRate`), which its data layer and the bridge use. The React pages (the portals, the punch page, the manager's React pieces) import `src/lib/payroll.ts` directly. The service worker caches `payroll.js`.
- **A change to payroll** is made in `payroll.ts` only, followed by `npm run build:payroll`. `tests/payroll/compare.test.mjs` runs the old page code (`tests/payroll/legacy.mjs`, a verbatim snapshot of the four pages' copies from main at 0a1ea33) and `payroll.js` on the same generated months and requires identical results ([tests.md](tests.md)); an intended pay change also updates the snapshot and says so in its PR.

- **`groupPunchesIntoShifts(entries)`** — one worker's punches, sorted by `ts`, split into shift buckets: each IN starts a bucket and the next OUT closes it. An overnight IN→OUT stays one shift. A bucket's `date` is the date of its first punch.
- **`analyzeDayPunches(entries, date, opts?)`** → `{hours, warnings, codes}`.
  - Pairs IN→OUT. **A pair's length comes from the punches' time stamps** (`ts`, in whole minutes as the clock showed them — `pairMins`), so a night across a clock change counts its real hours (Sat 2026-10-25 22:00 → 06:00 is 9 h, a night in late March 7 h) and an OUT punched 25 h after its IN counts 25 h (with the >12 h warning). Only when a stamp is missing or unreadable does it fall back to the `HH:MM` strings (a negative duration wraps past midnight). A punch with no `time` still leaves its pair out, as before.
  - With `opts.tr` (the manager app): warnings (English texts through the page's `tr()`, so in the chosen language): double IN, OUT without IN, a 0-minute shift, a shift over 12 h, an open shift; an open shift *today* counts up to now (from its stamp); durations go through `roundMins()` (`PAY_RULES.roundMinutes`, 0 = off); a time with no minutes reads as NaN in the fallback, as it always did there.
  - `codes` names each warning, in step with `warnings` (`WarnCode`: `double-in`, `out-no-in`, `zero`, `gt12`, `open` — no OUT, `open-today` — still at work): a page sorts warnings by their code, never by the translated text.
  - Without it (the portals and the punch page): only closed IN→OUT pairs count, `warnings` and `codes` stay empty, a time with no minutes reads as HH:00 in the fallback.
- **`PAY_RULES`** = `{regularMax:8, ot1Span:2, roundMinutes:0, otMult:1.25, shabbatMult:1.5}`.
- **`israeliDayPay(hours, rate, isSat)`** — Israeli law tiers:

  | Hours | Weekday | Saturday |
  |---|---|---|
  | first 8 | ×1.0 | ×1.5 |
  | next 2 | ×1.25 | ×1.75 |
  | beyond | ×1.5 | ×2.0 |
- **`effectiveRates(w, clientId, clients)`** (page wrapper `effectiveRates(w, clientId)`) — the most specific rate wins:
  1. `w.clientRates[clientId]` — the worker's own rate at this client (Accounting matrix);
  2. the worker's own rate — `hourlyRate` (or the older `regRate`), `otRate`, `shabbatRate` — from the worker modal or Accounting → Salary; it changes this worker only;
  3. the client's `defaultHourlyRate` / `defaultOtRate` / `defaultShabbatRate` — shared by every worker there without a rate of their own.
  
  Each of the three rates resolves on its own. Without an explicit value, OT = regular × 1.25 and Shabbat = regular × 1.5. `hasOtOverride` / `hasShabbatOverride` say whether an explicit value was found at any tier; `src` / `otSrc` / `shabbatSrc` say which tier (`override`, `worker`, `client`, or empty).- **`shiftPay(hours, w, isSat, clientId)`**:
  - Saturday — a flat Shabbat override if one exists, otherwise the Saturday law tiers;
  - weekday — with an OT override, `min(h,8) × reg + rest × ot`; otherwise the weekday law tiers.
  
  **Saturday = the shift's opening date is a Saturday** (`date.getDay() === 6`).
- **Daily pay** — `w.payMode === 'daily'` (picked per worker in the pay window, `savePayModal`): the worker is paid `dailyRate(w)` — their own rate (`wOwnRate`), read as ₪/day — once for every day worked, whatever the hours; no OT / Shabbat, and a client's (hourly) rate never applies. Every payroll figure prices a shift with **`shiftPayFor(hours, w, isSat, clientId, date, seenDays)`**: for a daily-paid worker the first shift of a day pays the day rate and later shifts that day pay 0 (`seenDays`, one Set per worker over the period); otherwise `shiftPay`. Days set by hand for a period (`w.dayOverrides[salaryPeriodKey(period)]`, `dayOverride`; keys `w:<Sunday>`, `m:<yyyy-mm>`, `all`) replace the counted days: pay = days × daily rate.
- **Client billing** — a separate figure that never changes worker pay: `clientBillPay(hours, client, isSat)` (`src/manager/accounting.ts`).
  - `fixed` → `hours × fixedBillRate`;
  - `daily` → `dailyBillRate` for a shift with hours (0 for an empty one), whatever its length;
  - `tiered` → the law tiers on `clientBillRate(client)` (`billRate`, else the old `defaultHourlyRate`), unless a flat `billOtRate` / `billShabbatRate` is set (applied like `shiftPay`).

### Salary

The rows come from `salaryEntriesFor(d, period)` → `{byWorker, byClient, byDay}` (byWorker entries: `worker, workerId, hours, days, regH, otH, shabH, total, travel, warns, warnCodes, clientHours, countedDays, daysUsed, supCom`); the accountant report reuses them. The worker's own "My Hours and Pay" on the punch page repeats the same steps for one month ([punch.md](punch.md)).

- **Calculation:**
  1. **A shift belongs to the period of its opening punch** (`periodShifts`): each worker's punches are paired over the whole loaded log with `groupPunchesIntoShifts`, and a shift is kept when its date (its first punch) falls in the period (`periodRange` / `inPeriod`: This Week from Sunday 00:00, This Month, `m:YYYY-MM` = [the 1st, the next 1st), all). So a night from Oct 31 22:00 to Nov 1 06:00 is 8 h in October and nothing in November, and a Saturday night into Sunday stays in the week it started. The punches a past month needs are loaded from the day before its 1st onwards (`periodPunchStart`), which covers both its first night and its last.
  2. Each shift goes through `analyzeDayPunches` (with the warnings and their codes).
  3. Per worker it sums hours, days worked and regular / OT / Saturday hours.
  4. **Pay** per shift = `shiftPayFor(h, worker, isSat, clientId of the shift's first punch, date, seen)`; a daily-paid worker's days set by hand for the period replace the counted ones (days × daily rate).
  5. **Travel** = `travelPay(w, days, months)`: `w.travelAllowance` ₪ × days worked (`daysUsed`) + `w.travelMonthly` ₪ once for each calendar month with a day worked. Travel is never part of the wage.
  6. For a worker with a supplier, `e.supCom` sums the supplier's own commission shift by shift (`supplierShiftCommission(sp.commission, h, date, e.supDays)`).
  7. In parallel, `byClient[clientId]` sums hours and `clientBillPay()` for shifts that have a `clientId`, and `byDay` the hours per day.
- **Rows: only the workers with shifts in the period.** The search icon opens a field (focused); it narrows the rows and the totals with them (`searchRows`); Billing and Alerts always use every row.
- **Totals** (`Figures`, above the table): Hours, Pay, Travel ₪, Total Pay (= pay + travel), an HR's My commission (`salaryTotals`).
- **Table** (`DataTable`): Worker (⚠ with the warnings when there are punches to check; the name opens the worker card), Hours, Regular / OT / Shabbat, Days, Rate (the own rate ₪/h or ₪/day; grey "client ₪x/h" when the client's applies — the client the worker had most hours at, `rateShown`; amber "set a daily rate" / "no rate"), Pay, Travel ₪, Total Pay (an HR's My commission), and the totals row; a column head sorts; 10 rows and Show more. A tap opens the row's details in a window (the hours split, Days with "punches: N" when set by hand, Rate, Pay, Travel, Bank — amber "no bank details" — an HR's commission, **Open card** and **Pay**); a double click opens the pay window. On a phone the rows are plates: a tap opens the details under the plate, a long press the pay window.
- **Select** (not for an HR): a Select button over the table, a tick column, the bulk bar's **Excel** / **PDF** export only the ticked workers' shifts.

### Pay window (`openPayModal(wid)`, `#payOverlay`, `PayWindow.tsx`)

The one place a worker's money is set — from a Salary row or the worker card's "Pay" button (so a worker without shifts in the period is set there); the worker modal and Upload hours carry no rate, pay mode, travel or bank field ([workers.md](workers.md)).

- **Who edits:** `canEditPay(w)` — the firm owner, a super-admin, staff with **Edit pay** (`staffPayEdit`), and an HR with Edit pay (`editPay`) for its own workers only ([hr.md](hr.md)). An HR with pay only opens its own workers' window read-only (every field off, no Save). `openPayModal` checks it (`payWindowAllowed`), then draws the window (`ManagerIsland.openPay`) and opens the overlay.
- **Fields:** Paid ₪/h | ₪/day (`payMode`; by the day hides OT / Shabbat and shows Days); Rate (the own rate; empty = the client's, by the hour only — its rate in the placeholder); **Days — <period>** for a daily worker (the days of the period picked, `dayOverride(w, salaryPeriodKey(period))`; empty = by the punches, shown as the placeholder; saved in `w.dayOverrides`); OT and Shabbat ₪/h (empty = law); Travel ₪/day (`travelAllowance`) and ₪/month (`travelMonthly`); bank, branch, account. **Save** writes `payPatch(w, form, key)` through `ManagerBridge.savePay` (`regRate` cleared, a field undefined removed), saves, closes and redraws.
- **Client rule** (`saveWorker`, [workers.md](workers.md)): adding a client that has a rate to an hourly worker with an own rate clears the own rate (`hourlyRate = 0`), so the new client's rate applies.

The per-client and per-supplier rate tables are not drawn (off since 2026-10-11); rates already set there still count — a worker's per-client override (`w.clientRates`, tier 1 of `effectiveRates`) and the per-supplier commission (`w.supplierRates`).

### Alerts (`accAlerts(d, entries, byClient)`)

Every accounting-only warning of the period's rows (none for an HR with pay only), as plates (red / amber bar, an icon) in three sections with their counts, paged by 10; the count sits on the tab.
- **Punches and shifts:** each `analyzeDayPunches` warning of a row (open shift — no OUT, double IN, OUT without IN, shift over 12h, 0-minute shift; amber for an open shift or over 12h, red for the rest), with the worker and the date; a shift still running today is left out. The level comes from the warning's code (`warnCodes`: `open` / `gt12` amber, `open-today` left out), so it is the same in every language. A tap opens the worker card.
- **Rates and pay:** a daily-paid worker without a daily rate ("No daily rate — pay 0"), an hourly worker with no rate of his own and none from the client ("No rate — pay 0"), no bank details (amber). A tap opens the pay window.
- **Clients:** a client with hours in the period and no billing rate for its mode (`hasBillRate`: fixed `fixedBillRate`, daily `dailyBillRate`, tiered `clientBillRate`) — "No billing rate — billed 0". A tap opens the client window.

### Supplier commission

- **The supplier's own rate:** `sp.commission` = `[{from:'YYYY-MM-DD'|'', mode:'hour'|'day', rate}]`, set in the supplier window by the firm owner or the super-admin ([suppliers.md](suppliers.md)); each entry applies from its date. `supplierRateOn(list, date)` → the newest entry whose `from` ≤ the date (an entry with an empty rate = back to the default; none → `null`). `supplierShiftCommission(list, h, date, seenDays)` → one shift: by the hour h × rate, by the day the rate once per day worked (`seenDays`), no rate `SUPPLIER_DEFAULT_RATE` (5) × h.
- **Worker override:** `supplierCommissionRate(w, sid)` returns `w.supplierRates[sid].rate` if set, otherwise the default; `supplierDayCommission(w, sid)` returns `w.supplierRates[sid].dayRate` if set, else `null`. `workerHasSupplierOverride(w, sid)`: a `rate`, or a `dayRate` > 0 on a daily-paid worker.
- **Commission:** `supplierCommission(w, e, sid)` — with an override, `commissionFor(w, e, hourRate, dayRate)` (paid by the day with a day rate: days × day rate; else hours × hour rate); otherwise `e.supCom`. `commissionFor` also prices the HR commission.

### Billing

Three sections; a section with no row is left out, and with none at all "Nothing payable or billable in this period".
- **Payable by supplier** (`supplierPayable`): per supplier, its workers' hours and `supplierCommission` (never a wage); only the suppliers with hours and a commission; workers without a supplier are left out. Excel / PDF icons in the head write its rows (`exportPayable`). A tap opens a window with the workers behind the sum (`supplierPayDetail`: hours and commission, the largest first, and the total).
- **Payable by HR** (`hrPayable`, owner / super-admin): per HR login, `commissionFor` with its `commissionRate` / `commissionDayRate` on the workers whose `hrId` is that HR; only HRs with hours and a commission; never a wage.
- **Billable by client** (`clientBillable`): per client, hours, mode (Fixed / Daily / Tiered) and the bill; only clients with hours and a bill. A tap opens its workers' hours there (`clientBillDetail`) and the total. It never shows anything about suppliers.

### Approvals

- **Hours approvals** (a month period only, never for an HR): see [hours-review.md](hours-review.md) → Accounting → Hours approvals.
- **Pending hours** (the approval queue): `loadPendingHours` lists the batches with status `pending` as plates (amber): the worker (or the file), "file · uploaded by · N shifts". For the owner / super-admin ✓ / ✕ on each and Select (Approve / Reject the ticked ones); `staff` sees the list read-only; a `supplier` is refused everywhere (the server checks the same). The list is read again when the batches change (`batchesKey`).
- **Approving:** `approveHoursBatch(id)` → `approvePendingHours` creates IN and OUT punches per row, with no GPS fields and the note `Imported from <file>` (a row whose date is not `dd/MM/yyyy` or whose time is not `HH:MM` makes no punch — never one at the moment of approval; `savePendingHoursBatch` refuses such a date in the first place); an OUT at or before the IN is a night shift and lands on the next day. The punches are written in batch writes of 450 (Firestore takes 500 writes per batch, so a long sheet works too), and the batch is marked approved only after all of them; a failure part way leaves it pending, and approving again rewrites the same punch ids (`imp-<batch>-<row>-<type>`), so nothing is duplicated; rejecting such a batch (`rejectPendingHours`) first deletes the punches already written (`punchesOfBatch`, in batch writes of 450), then marks it rejected. Approving an approved batch creates nothing. The app then reads the batch's punches (`punchesOfBatch`) and loads back to the earliest of them, so a past month shows up.
- **Rejecting:** `rejectHoursBatch(id)` (asks first; several: `bulkHoursBatches`, one question) → `rejectPendingHours` deletes any of the batch's punches left by an approve that failed part way, marks the batch rejected and creates nothing.
- Approving and rejecting store `decidedAt`; the sender sees the batch's status (worker card → Upload hours, [workers.md](workers.md); the supplier portal's My Hours). A supplier's batches (`supplierId`, uploaded by the supplier's name, "entered by hand") come in through the `supplier` function and are approved the same way.
- **Live count and status:** `watchHoursBatches()` (after the load, any manager-side login) keeps `__hoursBatches` from `listenPendingHours`. `updateHoursBadge()` shows the number of batches waiting on the Accounting tab (`#accPendingBadge`, hidden at 0); the worker card shows the worker's latest batch and its status (`HoursStatusChip` in `WorkerCard.tsx` over `latestHoursBatch`, redrawn live while the card is open); an open Accounting tab redraws.

Uploading the hours (worker card) is in [workers.md](workers.md).

## Exports

- **Salary export** (Salary's export icon, the `exports` section, not for an HR with pay only): a window with the period and CSV / Excel / PDF; it first loads the period's punches (`ensurePeriod`), then `exportSalary(api, format, period)` writes `salaryExportAoa(d, period, onlyIds)`:
  - one row per shift (`buildSalaryExportRows`) — Worker, Object/Client, Supplier, Date, First In, Last Out, Hours, Rate (the regular rate at the shift's client, or `<daily rate>/day`), Day Pay (`shiftPayFor`), Notes (the warnings); for a daily-paid worker with days set by hand, one extra row "Days set by hand: N (punches: M)" brings the total to N × rate; a total row (not in the CSV);
  - Excel — SheetJS (`ManagerBridge.xlsx()`, the page's `loadXlsx`), sheet "Salary", `salary-<period>.xlsx`; PDF — a print page in a new window; CSV — `salaryCsv` (`csvText` of `src/lib/utils.ts`: a UTF-8 BOM so Excel shows Hebrew and ₪, and a text cell that starts with = + - @ gets a leading apostrophe so it is never run as a formula; a number stays a number).
  - **Accountant report** (not for an HR) — `exportAccountant` → `accountantSheets(d, period, approvals, firm)`: `accountant-<period>.xlsx` with the sheet "Summary" (title, firm name, one row per worker from `salaryEntriesFor`: Worker, ID / Passport, Supplier, Pay mode, Rate, Days, Regular / Overtime / Shabbat / Total hours, Pay, Travel, Total, Bank, Branch, Account, Warnings, and a total row), "Shifts" (the rows of `buildSalaryExportRows`) and, for a month, "Hours approvals" (login, role, worker, date, status, proposed fix, comment, by, when — the approvals read afresh).
- **Hours of one worker:** `getWorkerMonthShifts(workerId, year, month, workerLog?)` → `[{date, inTime, outTime, hours}]` for shifts that *start* in that month. `exportShiftsDetail(workerId, year, month, 'excel'|'pdf')` writes Date / In / Out / Hours plus a total. Opened from the worker card ([workers.md](workers.md)).
- **Summary rows:** `exportPayable(api, rows, title, file, format)` writes rows the tab already computed (`{label, hours, rate, total}`, `payableAoa`) to Excel or PDF; it never recalculates.
- **Punch log CSV:** `exportPunchCSV()` — see [admin.md](admin.md).
- **Schedule week (Excel / PDF / JSON):** see [app-core.md](app-core.md).

## Today tab (`#sectionDashboard`)

Real-time attendance from the punch log and today's schedule. Never gated. **Drawn in React** (stage 6a): `src/manager/Today.tsx` with the logic in `src/manager/today.ts`, mounted into `#todayRoot` by the page's module (`src/manager/main.tsx`) ([build.md](build.md) → The manager's React pieces). The page's `renderDashboard()` (called by `render()` and `switchTab('dashboard')`, as before) only draws the greeting and calls `ManagerIsland.renderToday()`; the tab reads a snapshot from `ManagerBridge.today()`: every worker with today's shift (`shiftOn`), today's punch (`workerToday`), its site and its notices (`workerNotices` — they still belong to the Workers tab, so they are worked out on the page, once), the suppliers, today's punches with their site (`punchSite`), and whether the login may add workers. A tap on a worker runs the page's `openWorkerCardById`, a notice chip its `openNotices`, a supplier `switchTab('suppliers')`.

**Greeting** (redesign stage 5; in the title bar since V7, [app-core.md](app-core.md) → The V7 frame): "Good morning / Good afternoon / Good evening / Good night, <first name>" by the device's local time (5–12, 12–17, 17–21, 21–5; `partOfDay`, `greetingHtml` in `src/lib/greeting.ts`, drawn by the React frame's `ShellGreeting`; the page's `renderGreeting` gives it the name and the weather), with a small animated icon after the name (`GREET_ICONS`: sunrise, sun, sunset, moon with stars), after the date. The icon follows the real weather at the firm's main site with the temperature (redesign stage 12): `loadWeather` asks the `weather` function (docs/backend.md) once per 30 minutes or when the super-admin opens another firm, and `weatherIconHtml` draws clouds, rain, thunder, snow or fog (`WX_ICONS`) and "14°" (`.gtemp`); clear weather and any failure keep the time-of-day icon. **Notifications** (redesign stage 5): the bell in the title bar (the React frame's `ShellBell`, manager-side logins only — `refreshBell` hands it the unread count; a red count that pulses, the bell swings while something is unread). `notifList()` builds the list, newest first, each with a steady id (prefixed with the firm): an ID / passport or a document (visa, permit, contract, other) expired or expiring, a contract ended or ending, no punch on today's shift (the same rules as the worker plate's notices — [workers.md](workers.md)), hours batches waiting (`__hoursBatches`, with Accounting), and a client login that approved every day of this month (`refreshNotifApprovals`, the hours function's list at most every 5 minutes, not for HR). - **On shift** (`onsite`) — workers whose last punch of the last 24 hours is IN, including a night shift opened before midnight within the last 16 hours (an older lone IN is a forgotten OUT, not on shift; `ManagerBridge.today().recent`: the last 24 hours' punches, which also pair a night shift's OUT with its IN for the hours; Late, No punch and Today's Log stay on today's punches).
- **Late** (`late`) — workers whose first IN today came more than 5 minutes after today's planned start (`shiftOn`); the sub line shows the IN time and how late.
- **No punch** (`missing`) — today's shift started more than 15 minutes ago and there is no punch, or the worker punched OUT more than 10 minutes before the shift's end; how late on the right in red.
- **Notices** (`notices`) — every active worker with notices (`workerNotices`, the same as the worker plate: documents, contract, no punch…), urgent first; a tap opens the notices sheet.
- **Today's Log** (`log`) — today's punches, newest first: IN in green, OUT in red, the site, the time, an amber mark on a GPS-suspicious punch.
- **Workers by supplier** (`suppliers`) — a headcount line per supplier that has workers (a tap opens Suppliers); hidden when none; no pay data.

## Super-admin cross-firm summary

Admin → Firms → "Payable summary (all firms)" (a toggle in the React Firms section, `ManagerBridge.firmsPayable()` → the page's `firmsPayableRows()`; the rows are drawn by `src/manager/Firms.tsx`).
- For each cached firm, `fetchForTenant('loadSchedule'|'loadPunchLog', tenantId)` reads that firm with an explicit `asTenantId`, so the firm being viewed does not change. Punches: this month plus the week before.
- It computes this week's hours and pay per firm with the canonical functions (the punches paired over all it read, a shift kept in the week it opened; `shiftPayFor`; a daily-paid worker's days set by hand for this week count, as in Salary) and shows a plate per firm (hours and pay, "(load failed)" for a firm it could not read) with the grand total on the section's line.
- The client tier of `effectiveRates()` reads the global `clients`, so while a firm's pay is computed its own clients stand in for the loaded firm's, and are swapped back right after.
