# Hours review (`src/hours-review/`, the `hours` function)

The screen is drawn in the Odinson Glass look (redesign stage 10): glass tabs and month arrows, the status line as a plate with a coloured bar, each day a plate with its state as the bar (`--c`) and as a pill (`.hrv-pill`, `HRV_STATES[st].pill`: Waiting, Approved, Fixed, Changed, Disputed, Fix proposed, Rejected — stage 13) and the hours in the figure green. Its styles fall back to plain values where a page lacks the design tokens.

One screen, one Cloud Function and one store for every login that reviews its workers' hours: a **client**, a **supplier** and an **HR** — each in the manager app's **Hours** tab (a client's and a supplier's through `src/manager/Portal.tsx`, an HR's `HoursTab`). The same ticks, the same per-day actions, the same code (per user request 2026-10-11: "one template and one set of functions for hours").

## Ticks (from the flat rights, claim `rt` — `hour_ticks`, `HOUR_OF`)

`HOUR_PERMS` in `functions-auth/core.py` (the Users list shows a login's hours ticks from its flat rights, `HOUR_RIGHTS` in `src/manager/Users.tsx`), each given by one right — hours by **Hours**, approve / dispute / fix by **Approve / Dispute / Fix hours**, schedule by **Schedule**, export by **Export**:

- **hours** — see the month's shifts (in / out / hours per day);
- **approve** — approve a day, several ticked days at once (Select for a login with this tick — owner 2026-10-13: the Select button at the right of the Total line above the days; after it the same button reads "Select all" (then "Select none") and a tick box shows on every day still waiting / changed / whose fix was rejected; a tap on the day or its box ticks it, the day's own buttons stay; with a tick the glass bar shows — its Approve sends their lines in one `approve` (`approveSelected` in `HoursReview.tsx`), ✕ clears the ticks), or the whole month at once;
- **dispute** — dispute a day with a comment;
- **fix** — propose the right in / out of a day (with a comment); applied only when a manager accepts it;
- **schedule** — the workers' planned shifts;
- **export** — the month to Excel.

Set in the login's permission template (Admin → Users → Templates, a super-admin; [admin.md](admin.md) → Users pane) and shown read-only in the user window's **Hours** block (`src/manager/Users.tsx`), for HR, supplier and client (`HOUR_ROLES`). The default HR, Supplier and Client templates have Hours and Approve / Dispute / Fix hours (the HR one Schedule too). The login reply and the Users list carry the login's `rights`; a change ends the user's sessions like any rights change. The manager app reads them from the ID token at start (`firestore-backend.js` `rights()`).

Whose workers: a client — the workers at its site (`punches.clientId`); a supplier — its own (`workers.supplierId`); an HR — the workers assigned to it (`workers.hrId`), or every worker of the firm when its "Only own workers" is off (claim `own`). "Only own workers" closes the user form: an HR may untick it, a supplier and a client always have it on (locked).

## The `hours` function (`functions-auth/hours.py` → `hours_action(db, caller, body)`)

`caller` = the ID token's `uid, role, tenantId, clientId, supplierId, own` and its flat rights (`rights`, `core.caller_rights`; `main.py`). A superadmin passes `tenantId` in the body. `scope_of(caller)` gives `(kind, ownerId)`: `client`/clientId, `supplier`/supplierId, `hr`/uid; any other role → "Not available for this login". A missing tick → "Not allowed for this login". Never returns a wage, a rate, a supplier, GPS, notes, documents or a token.

- `load {month}` → `{owner: {kind, id, name, object}, perms, month, workers, punches?, days?}` — `workers` `{id, name}` (+ `schedule` / `dates` with the schedule tick, `portal._week` / `_dates`; a worker who worked here this month and has since moved is listed too); with the hours tick, the scope's `punches` of the month with a day of margin (`{id, workerId, type, time, date, ts}`) and `days` (the stored day entries).
- `approve {month, lines}` → each line `"<worker>|<day>|<ins>|<outs>"` (a day's shifts comma-joined; ≤ 3000, the scope's workers only — for a client, the workers with punches at its site that month) stored as `approved`. Dispute and fix take only the same workers (else "Bad day").
- `dispute {month, workerId, day, line, comment}` → `disputed` with the comment; an empty comment takes a dispute back.
- `fix {month, workerId, day, line, shift?, inT, outT, comment}` → `fix` (HH:MM checked); `shift` picks one of the day's shifts (0 = the first; out of range → "Bad shift"), stored with its old times `oldIn` / `oldOut`, which the accept looks for; the day's line then gets that shift's new times.
- `sign {month, pages}` (a **client** login with the hours and approve ticks; any other scope → "Not available for this login") → the month signed: `pages` are 1–12 base64 JPEGs (≤ 2 MB each, checked by their header) drawn by the portal; at least one day of the month must be approved ("Approve the month first"); `jpeg_pdf` wraps them into one A4 PDF (no PDF library), stored through the Admin SDK at `tenants/{t}/clients/{clientId}/documents/hours-<month>-signed.pdf` (a new signature replaces it), and `signed {path, at, byName, pages}` is merged into the month's document. The reply, like `load` (a client's, with the hours tick), carries only `signed {at, byName}` — never the path. `main.py` passes `storage.bucket` as `hours_action(..., bucket)`.
- Managers only (`_is_manager`: owner, super-admin, staff with Accounting in its flat rights): `list {month}` → every login's document of the month (an HR's with `ownOnly`); `resolve {docId, key, accept}` → a proposed fix accepted (the shift's in / out punches matching the old line get the new times as Israel time — an out before the in moves to the next day —, and the out's `day` / `date` follow it; a punch the fix leaves at its time is not written; in a shift with no IN fixed into a night one (22:00–06:00) the worker's real OUT stays where it is and the new IN goes on the evening before, so the shift (dated by its IN) is now the day before's: the fixed entry is written under that day's key — in the month before's document when the day is the 1st — with that day's line paired from the punches as the review pairs them (`_day_lines`), and the reviewed day keeps the fixed entry with its remaining shifts' line, or loses its entry when none is left, so both rows show Fixed (the reply carries the new `key`); a fixed shift that would take in another punch of the worker, bounds included, or sit inside another shift (an IN before it whose OUT comes after it) → "The fixed shift overlaps other punches — edit them in the punch log"; `editedBy: 'approval'`; status `fixed`, the line updated — its shifts in the order they open, as the review lists them, so the row shows Fixed) or rejected (`rejected`); `resolvedBy` / `resolvedAt` stored. A shift with no OUT (or no IN) gets the missing punch, made from the one it has (`workerId`, `workerName`, `hrId`, `clientId`, `site`; the new time, `day` / `date`, `editedBy: 'approval'`). A shift whose punches can't be matched (several shifts, edited since) → "edit them in the punch log". The accept re-reads the proposal and writes the punches and the entry in one transaction, so two accepts at once never both move the punches (the second gets "Nothing to resolve").

Store: `tenants/{t}/hourApprovals/{kind}_{ownerId}_{YYYY-MM}` = `{kind, ownerId, ownerName, month, days: {"<workerId>|<YYYY-MM-DD>": {status, line, comment?, inT?, outT?, byName, at, resolvedBy?, resolvedAt?}}, signed?: {path, at, byName, pages}}` — written only by the function (an approve / dispute / fix reads `days`, changes it and writes it back whole in one transaction — `_update_days` — so two reviews of the same document at once lose nothing; `signed` is kept); managers read it (`firestore.rules`).

## The screen in the new stack (`src/hours-review/`)

The screen of a client's and a supplier's Hours tab and of an HR's Hours tab, all inside the manager app (built in stages 3, 4 and 6e of [react-migration-plan.md](../archive/docs/react-migration-plan.md)). The old pages' copy, `hours-review.js`, was removed with stage 6e.

- `hours.ts` — the data: `HOURS_FN_URL`; the reply types (`HoursData`: `month, perms, owner, workers, punches, days`); `monthKey(offset)` / `monthStart(key)` (date-fns); `can(data, tick)`; `monthDays(data)` (the shift pairing of `src/lib/payroll.ts`, a row per worker and day with its `line` — the same lines as the owner's `hourDayLines`); `dayState(data, row)`; `PICKABLE`; `STATES` (colour, label, pill); `planned(worker, date)`.
- `HoursReview.tsx` — `HoursReview({call, hideOwner, sign?})`; `call(body)` posts to the `hours` function (the page's own, with its ID token). The state (month, tab, data) lives outside React (`useSyncExternalStore`), so a language change redraws without a new load; `hoursReviewReload()` reloads, `useHoursOwner()` gives the header its client. Tabs **Hours** / **Schedule** (ui Tabs) by their ticks; month arrows (no future months). **Hours**: the Figures (approved, waiting, disputed or changed, fixes proposed); the status plate ("N of M days approved" …, **Export**, **Approve the month** — asks first in an Alert Dialog); the total; **Select** (`useSelection` over the pickable days: a tap ticks a day, the bulk bar's **Approve** sends their lines in one `approve`); a Section per worker (name, days, hours), paged by 10 (`PagedList`), a Plate per day — the date, the state pill, the shifts, the hours, the bar in the state's colour, the state's note under it; its ⋮ holds Approve this day / Dispute this day / Propose a fix by the ticks, and a tap opens the fix (else the dispute). The dispute and fix windows (`Window`) have no buttons: closing one sends (dispute: empty = take it back; fix: only when a time differs from the punches; a day with several shifts first picks the shift). **Schedule**: a Section per worker with a Table of its planned days (today in accent). With `sign` (a client) see Signing the month below. Plates and controls carry `data-day-key` / `data-state` / `data-testid` for the browser tests.

### Signing the month (a client, stage 5c)

- With `sign={signer}` (a client's Hours tab passes the person's name) and the approve tick: once every day of the month is approved or its fix accepted, the status plate shows **Sign the month** (primary; **Sign again** once signed, and "Signed <date> — <name>" under it); "Approve the month" that leaves no day open opens the same window at once.
- The window (`SignWindow`, a `Window` with one primary): the days and hours of the month, a note that the signed PDF is kept with the client's documents at the firm, the shared `SignaturePad`; **Sign and save** (off until something is drawn) → `signedMonthPages(data, days, png, signer)` (`signed-pdf.ts`) draws A4 pages at 150 dpi on white — the title, the month, the client and object, a row per day (date, worker, shifts, hours, state pill), the total, "I approve the hours of this month.", the signature, the signer and the time — as JPEGs, sent as `sign`. Only what the screen shows a client goes in: no pay, rates, suppliers, GPS, files or tokens. Closing the window signs nothing.
- The owner: Accounting → Hours approvals shows a client's signed month on its plate ("Signed <date> — <name>", **Signed PDF** → `openSignedHours(path)`: `firestore-backend.js` `storageUrl(path)` in a new tab; the managers' Storage read in `storage.rules`).

Where it runs:

- **A client login** — its one tab, Hours, inside the manager app ([portals.md](portals.md) → Client login).
- **A supplier login** — its **Hours** tab inside the manager app (`HoursReview`, [portals.md](portals.md) → Supplier login), shown with an hours or schedule tick.
- **Manager app, an HR login** — its **Hours** tab (`#hoursRoot` → `HoursTab`, `#accSecReview`: the React `HoursReview` over the page's `hoursCall`, `ManagerBridge.hoursCall`), shown with an hours or schedule tick (`hrHourReview()`).

## Accounting → Hours approvals (the owner)

The Approvals tab's **Hours approvals** (`ApprovalsTab`, Accounting.tsx; a month period only, never for an HR): every login's document of the month (`ManagerBridge.hourApprovals(month)` → the page's `hourApprovalsOf(month)` → `list`; kept for a minute per month so a redraw on each new punch doesn't ask the server — opening the tab, an accept / reject and the accountant report read afresh); it first waits for the first load of the punches (`__punchSynced`, set by `syncPunchesFromGS`), so a day is never compared with a punch log still loading, and every redraw (each punch change while Accounting is open) compares again with the punches in memory. One plate per login (kind: Client / Supplier / HR, `approvalDocsShown`) with "N of M days approved", a client's signed month ("Signed <date> — <name>", **Signed PDF**) and each day that needs a look — disputed (comment), fix proposed (old → new times, comment, **Accept** (asks first) / **Reject** → `ManagerBridge.resolveFix`), fix rejected, changed after approval. "Changed" and the day count come from `hourApprovalSummary(d, doc, month)` over `hourDayLines(d, doc, month)` (`src/manager/accounting.ts`): the scope's punches in the owner's punch log paired into the same lines as the review (supplier: its workers; HR: its workers, or all when its own-only is off — `ownOnly`, which `list` adds to an HR's document; client: punches at the client). After an accept the punch listener brings the moved punches.

The accountant report (a month) has a sheet **Hours approvals**: login, role, worker, date, status, proposed fix, comment, by, when.
