What is where

Top right: one button with a globe and the current language name; a tap opens the list from the bottom: every language in its own script and in English, a search on top, the current one ticked. However many languages are added, the screen keeps one button. Under the greeting, a plate “Your schedule was updated · View” when the manager changed shifts. Under the Punch button, two large tiles: “My Schedule” (next shift; an “Updated” tag when something changed) and “My Hours” (the month's hours in green). A tap on a tile opens the schedule or the hours.
