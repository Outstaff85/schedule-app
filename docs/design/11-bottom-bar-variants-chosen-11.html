7. Notch

The bar is docked to the bottom with a smooth notch; the active icon rises into a colored circle above the notch, and the notch moves with it.

8. Light from below

A soft light rises from the bottom edge under the active item and the icon glows; a bright strip along the very edge. The light flows to the tapped item.

9. Filled icon

All icons are outlines; the active one fills with a color gradient, its name and a short dash appear under it. Calm and clean.

10. Glass column

Behind the active item, a glass tile the full height of the bar with a colored line at the bottom; it moves over with a spring.

11. A color per section

Each section has its own color: Today cyan, Schedule lilac, Workers green, Accounting amber, More pink. The active one colors its icon, a tile behind it and the line at the bottom. (Chosen.)

12. Minimal

Icons only, no labels; the active one a little bigger with a small uppercase name under it and a thin white line on the edge. The most room for the screen.
