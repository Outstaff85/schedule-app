Today is the live attendance screen and the first tab for everyone except HR.

- Greeting by the device's local time with an animated sun or moon icon; the icon follows the real weather at the firm's main site.
- Four figures on top: **On shift**, **Late**, **No punch**, **Notices**. A figure is a filter: a tap shows only its section and writes "Showing: … · Show all" under the figures.
- Sections fold with the arrow at their right: On shift, Late, No punch, Notices (worker plates with chips), Today's Log (newest first) and Workers by Supplier (headcount only, no pay).
- The bell in the header opens the notifications panel: newest first, an icon in the importance color, one detail line, the time; a glowing cyan dot marks unread.
- Today's rules: Late = first IN more than 5 minutes after the planned start; No punch = shift started over 15 minutes ago with no punch.
