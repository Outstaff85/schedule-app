The Accounting tab: payroll and billing. It is gated by the accounting permission; an HR sees only its own workers' pay and its commission.

- Top: a glass period switcher (‹ This Week ›), three figures (Hours, Payroll, To approve) and four tabs **Salary / Billing / Approvals / Alerts**; Alerts and Approvals carry a red count.
- **Salary:** a plate per worker with hours and total pay; a row opens its details and the **Pay** window (₪/h or ₪/day, OT, Shabbat, travel, bank). A total row covers every item. On a phone the tables become cards, with a list/cards toggle.
- **Billing:** payable by supplier, payable by HR, billable by client (hours, mode Fixed or Tiered, total).
- **Approvals:** hours batches waiting for the owner (✓ and ✕, or Select and Approve) and what clients, suppliers and HR approved, disputed or proposed to fix.
- **Alerts:** accounting-only warnings as plates in three sections: Punches and shifts, Rates and pay, Clients; a tap opens the place to fix it.
- Real screens of all four tabs are in the "Accounting tabs" card.
