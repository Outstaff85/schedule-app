The Workers tab: the main list of the app.

- Header: title with the count and one glass create button, **New worker** (owner, 2026-10-26): its main part adds a worker by scan, its arrow opens Scan passport and Enter by hand; Select sits above the plates.
- No status tabs over the list (owner, 2026-10-26): the Status and Documents filters do that job.
- Search by name, phone, ID or passport number, or client; a **Filters** button opens Status, Documents, Client, Supplier and Group by, and each set filter shows as a removable chip.
- A plate: the bar (red inactive or urgent, amber vacation or notices, green otherwise), the full name, a "+N" chip, the first notice chip on its own line, "site · today's punch" under the name, month hours in green left of ⋮.
- Tap opens the worker card; double click or long press edits; swipe left shows Edit and Delete; Delete shows the Undo snack.
- A tap on a notice chip opens the notices sheet, one plate per notice with its action: Upload, Renew, Extend, Call or WhatsApp.
