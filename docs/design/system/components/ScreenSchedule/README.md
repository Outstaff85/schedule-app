The weekly roster.

- Header: the week navigator (‹ dates ›) in glass; a tap on the dates opens the date picker; the export icon opens Excel / PDF / JSON of the shown week.
- Site tabs under it: All sites and one tab per client; they filter the plates.
- A plate per worker: short name, "site · N shifts" and seven day marks (green work, blue night shift, amber vacation, grey off; today ringed); the week's hours in green; the bar is green with shifts and amber on vacation.
- A tap opens the seven days under the plate; a day opens the shift editor: work or off, start and end, and **Applies to**: every weekday or only that date. A date-only day offers "Back to the weekly schedule".
- Lists page by 10.
