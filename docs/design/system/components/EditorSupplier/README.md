The supplier editor: the supplier company, what the firm owes it as commission, and the portal login its staff use.

## Head
The supplier's color, name, "N workers · ₪X this month" (the commission payable) and a ⋮ with **Delete**.

## Sections, in this order
1. **Company:** name; company number (ח.פ) and phone in a pair; contact person; bank, branch and account number.
2. **Commission** (editing only): the month's figures (hours, commission, workers); a switch **Per hour / Per day**; the rate and the date it applies from; a note that a worker's own commission in Accounting wins over this rate.
3. **Portal login:** username and password, and what the login may see (tabs Dashboard, My Workers, My Hours, Approvals; whether it sees its workers' pay).
4. **Workers:** mini plates with each worker's hours and **Add workers**.

## Behaviour
- One primary **Save** at the bottom.
- Deleting a supplier asks nothing: the plate collapses behind the Undo snack, and after 5 seconds every worker loses this supplier and its per-supplier rate.
- A supplier never sees client billing, GPS, punch-link tokens or file links.
