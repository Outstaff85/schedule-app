# Scanner rebuild plan

The approved plan for the new document scanner: automatic edge detection, a live frame in the camera, two documents in one photo split into two pages, a queue for multi-page uploads, all of it on the new stack (`src/scan/`). The current scanner is described in docs/scan.md; it stays in place until stage S4 and is deleted only in S5, with the owner's permission.

Status: **S1 done** (the sign-in check, the server's document finding, the `detectAlign` prompt without corners, the MRZ checks). **S2 + S2b done** (owner, 2026-10-18: the whole lab scanner goes in, the old one is left as it is until it is switched off): the scan window `src/scan/` (docs/scan.md → The scan window) — the lab's live frame, autocapture and upright turn on the phone, the server's cut, photos and PDFs — is what Scan opens. **S3, S4, S5 done** (owner, 2026-10-19: "do everything from S3"): the reading is `src/scan-process/` (docs/scan.md → Reading), proven equal to the old one by `tests/scan/compare.test.mjs`; it runs in the scan window on Done; `scan-pipeline.js`, its scan window, corner editor and queue / review screens are gone. Left: S7 (the document type model — prepared, waiting for the page copies). Each stage is merged only with the owner's OK.

## Models (chosen after tests on the owner's photos)

- **DocAligner** `fastvit_sa24` heatmap model (Apache-2.0) — the four corners of a document. fp32 83 MB on the server; **fp16 42 MB** on the phone (the same accuracy; int8 is broken — not used). Input `img` [1,3,256,256] RGB/255, output `heatmap` [1,4,128,128]; a corner = the centroid of the largest blob over 0.3; confident when every corner's peak is ≥ 0.5.
- **OpenCV candidates** next to it (multi-Canny contours, Otsu color segmentation, GrabCut), scored by side contrast in Lab, aspect 1.25–2.0, refined to the gradient lines. DocAligner wins when it is confident and OpenCV found fewer than two documents.
- **MRZScanner** (mrzscanner-docsaid, detection + recognition ONNX, ~0.5 s) for passports, with the ICAO check digits (7,3,1) on the passport number, birth date and expiry.
- Rejected: U²-Net (not needed), DocTr / DocGeoNet / UVDoc / page-dewarp (they spoil the image), FastMRZ (AGPL + Tesseract), int8 models.

## Stages

### S1 — server

- **Done:** `preprocess_scan` and `fill_form_101` (`functions/main.py`) require a manager-side login's ID token (`functions/gate.py`; [backend.md](backend.md) → Cloud Functions) — before, anyone with the URL could call them.
- **Done:** the server's document finding — `preprocess_scan` with `find` (`functions/docfind.py`, [scan.md](scan.md) → Server-side finding): the accurate model in fp32 (then the light one, then both inside a 10 % margin), the sides pulled onto the edges by OpenCV, several documents, photos on a white page, the face turn, the models read from Storage; 2 GiB, cpu=2, concurrency=2, max_instances=10. On the owner's 36 photos: the same documents as the lab (n and its crop → ID, ספח, bank card), about 0.5–1.5 s a photo on 2 CPU threads.
- Detection: DocAligner + OpenCV as above; **two documents in one photo** → two quads; after one document is found it is masked and searched again (photos where the second document was missed); a document inside a screenshot frame is searched inside the frame.
- **Several documents on one photo** (an ID, its ספח, a bank card…; up to 4): each next one is looked for on the photo with every found one painted over — by the accurate model, then the light one — then with a 10 % margin around the photo, then in the strip beside the first; it runs even when the accurate model does not confirm the first. The owner's tablecloth screenshot, cropped to its photo: ID, ספח and bank card all found. A page that is not wanted (a bank card, a part of an old Form 101 in a screenshot) is classified "other" by Haiku and **left unsaved by default** on the review screen (the owner can keep it).
- **Speed of the search for more documents:** the ways of one step (accurate / light model, painted, with the margin, the strips) run one after another and stop at the first that finds one — on the phone that is faster than running them all at once (tried: 6.0 → 8.8 s on a three-document photo, since the slow accurate model then runs every way and the threads share the CPU). On the server the ways of a step can go as one batch through the model.
- **Photos on a white page** (owner, 2026-10-18: uploads are usually white pages — a form's page, a PDF, a phone screenshot — with photos of the documents in them): every band of rows mostly not white (at least 12 % high) is a photo, its sides from the columns mostly not white inside it; each photo is cut out and goes through on its own, and their documents make one list of pages. A photo with no document frame in it is a document already cut to its edges and becomes a page whole. On the owner's screenshots: the tablecloth screenshot → ID, ספח, bank card; a two-photo Form 101 screenshot → ID and ספח; plain photos untouched. Shown in the scanner lab; the server does the same.
- **Two documents on one photo** (a passport and its ספח): after the first, the accurate model looks again on the photo with the first painted over in the background's colour, then with a 10 % margin of that colour around it (a document cut off by the photo's edge then lies whole inside and its corners land on the edge), then in the widest strip left beside it; a frame overlapping the first by over 30 % is the same one. On the owner's photos: 6 of 6 two-document photos split, no extra page on any one-document photo (the light model alone finds the second in 1 of 5, so the split belongs to the accurate model — the server). The phone shows the first page at once; the second appears when the server returns it. Shown in the scanner lab, where the accurate model is on the phone.
- **Done:** MRZ check digits for passports — the number, the birth date and the expiry are taken from MRZ line 2 when each one's check digit is right (`functions-auth/ai.py`, `_expiry_from_mrz`).
- **Haiku only for pages without a face** (owner, 2026-10-18): a page whose face set it upright (ID card, passport) is not sent to `aiDetectAlign` — its turn is the phone's, its kind comes with its read; a page with no face (the ספח, a contract) still goes to Haiku for its kind and turn. One decides the turn, never both, so the two never fight. **Done:** the `detectAlign` prompt dropped the corners (kind and turn only) and `tests/auth/ai_golden.json` changed with it; the old scanner, being switched off, keeps working without them (it only turns the page, no AI crop — owner, 2026-10-18).
- The models stored as fp16 (deploy size limit) or loaded from Cloud Storage at cold start.
- **Settings for parallel pages:** the function is CPU-bound (ONNX + OpenCV), not an AI wait — `cpu=2`, `memory=2GiB`, `concurrency=2`, `max_instances=10`; no shared per-request state, so several pages run on several instances at once.
- Tests in `tests/auth`/`functions` for the detection (the quads on fixed synthetic images), the MRZ check digits and the auth check.

### S2 — React scan window (`src/scan/`)

- The corner editor with the found frames (several frames on one photo), a loupe while dragging a corner.
- **Queue screen for uploads (photos and PDFs) — parallel:**
  - after the files are picked, every page is sent to `preprocess_scan` at once, **at most 4 in flight** (phone memory); PDF pages are rendered one by one and each goes out as soon as it is rendered;
  - the preparation of the images (rotation, downscale, PDF → images) runs in a Web Worker, so the screen never freezes;
  - each page appears in the queue as soon as its frame is found (a green mark); the user corrects page 1 while the rest are still being detected; a photo with two documents becomes two pages in the queue;
  - on Done, the AI reads, the digit verification and the MRZ of all pages start at once (`Promise.all`) and are merged in page order (CLAUDE.md → Parallel AI calls).
- Through the shared upload helpers (`filesToUploadPages` / `downscaleForUpload` / `fileForStorage`).
- **Background threads (Web Workers, no special headers needed):** the image preparation (rotation, downscale, PDF → images, 2–4 pages at once), the detection model and the perspective warp each run off the page's thread, so the screen and the queue stay smooth while pages are processed.

### S2b — camera with a live frame

Proven on the owner's Android phone with the scanner lab (see below, 2026-10-18): live frame 13 frames/s, the screen at 60 frames/s, the shot shown in 0.18 s.

- **Models on the phone:** the light DocAligner model (5 MB) for the live frame, the accurate one (42 MB, fp16 weights with fp32 math — a plain fp16 conversion gives zeros on WebGPU) for the shot. On the first tap of Scan both download in the background over any network, with the progress shown; **the first time, scanning is enabled only once they are ready**; then they come from the cache (Android: Background Fetch; iOS: a resumable download). Both are warmed up at load.
- **Changed 2026-10-19 (owner: "the scan must be the lab's — copy it as it is"): the site's scanner is the lab's own page**, copied into `scan/` and opened in the scan window (docs/scan.md → The scan window); the light and the accurate model both run on the phone, as in the lab. Before that, briefly, a port of it ran there: in its own thread, as in the lab — it finds and cuts a shot's and a picked photo's documents (`src/scan/find.ts`), the server only when the phone cannot (docs/scan.md → The scan window). What follows was the first way (2026-10-18), kept for the record: **on the phone only the light model**: the accurate model runs on the server (S1), not on the phone. The phone downloads ~12 MB — the light model (4.4 MB), the face finder (0.2 MB) and one ONNX Runtime (1.30, ~7 MB) — instead of ~56 MB: ~5 s on 4G the first time, ~1–2 s from the cache after. The shot is shown at once, cut by the live frame (0.2–4.5 % off the accurate corners on the owner's photos), and the page is sent **whole with the live frame's corners**; the server finds the exact corners (accurate model, fp32, plus OpenCV on the edges), cuts the page and reads it, so the stored and read page is the accurate one. The lab keeps the accurate model on the phone to compare.
- **Where they run** — tried in this order, each way dropped if it fails or stays silent for 25 s, nothing remembered between opens (owner, 2026-10-22: every open tries the best way first, so a one-off failure never keeps the phone on a slower way); every failure is kept on the device with its reason — memory, hang, GPU or another error (`noteFail`, `failKind`; the last 8, `localStorage.scanWayFails`) — and shown when the page opens (`showFails`), with the phone's memory, in the lab's **Errors** block — kept apart from the loading log under it (owner, 2026-10-24): every failure, a page error, a GPU that gets the check wrong or a model that could not start goes there with its time (`logErr`; the lines from `scan-core.js` and the worker are sorted by `logAny`), the rest of the work to the loading log; the log also says when the page is not cross-origin isolated (each CPU thread then runs on one core):
  1. the live model on the **GPU** (WebGPU, ONNX Runtime 1.30) in two background threads, the accurate model on the **CPU** (ONNX Runtime 1.22) in its own background thread;
  2. the live model on the CPU in several single-thread background threads (half the cores, at most 3), frames handed to them in turn, plus the accurate model's thread.
  Before the GPU is used, each model is run on a drawn card with known corners in both memory layouts (NCHW / NHWC); a GPU that gets it wrong is not used (seen on the owner's phone: the GPU ran the light model right only in NCHW with 1.30, and never the accurate one). CPU threads inside a background thread hang on Android Chrome, so the CPU uses several single-thread background threads instead; no cross-origin isolation headers are needed.
- **A document lying on another** (an ID card on its ספח, a passport on a page; owner, 2026-10-22): the painting over hides it with the one under it, so every document found is also cut flat, not turned, and looked at on its own with nothing painted (`findInside`, camera shots and picked files alike): a document of a document's shape covering 3–60 % of it is another page, its corners taken back onto the photo through the cut's homography (cut, upright-turned and sent for training like any other page; `scripts/scan-train/attempts.py` does not paint over the document it lies on, and a deleted one teaches nothing). One level only; 5 documents a photo at most. Tried on the owner's ספח photo: the ID card lying on it came out as its own page.
- **The accurate model first** (owner, 2026-10-22): when the page opens, the accurate model's thread is started alone on the CPU (`ensureCap`, `capThread`), and picked photos and PDFs can go in as soon as it is up; the live frame's models are loaded after it — in the background once nothing is being worked on, or when the camera starts — and the pool ways (GPU live, CPU threads) share that thread for the shot (`poolEngine(…, shared)`): one copy of the accurate model, the same models and memory as before, in another order. A thread of it that stops answering is started again and logged.
- **Picked files** (photos, PDFs; owner, 2026-10-22): one picture per thread (`analyzePicture`: its photos inside a white page, each one's corners, the other documents on it found in turn with the ones found painted over, the documents lying on them, the cut and the upright turn — no screen), the pages shown strictly in the files' order (`showPicture`). The engine's capture thread takes them; when a pick has a second picture, **one** more thread with the accurate model is started in the background (`growFiles`: not on a phone under 4 GB of memory or 4 cores; kept for the next picks) and takes pictures as soon as it is up — a single picture never waits for it (owner's phone, 2026-10-22: two more copies of the model loading at once made the engine's own capture thread stop answering, and one photo took 30 s). A thread that fails or stops answering hands its picture to the main engine. No GPU for files. While a pick is worked on, the picture next to go on the list is shown with the frame of each document as it is found and what is going on ("Finding the document… / Found — cutting it out…"), as a camera shot is; the pages join the list below as each picture is done. The log gives the time and the threads used, and for each picture where its time went (`pictureTimer`, owner 2026-10-24): opening it, the first document, looking for more (the last look, which finds none, included), a document lying on one, the cuts with the upright turn — each in ms with its model runs — then the lab's readouts. The lab's zone readout says "no passport zone found" for two lines at the foot of a page that is no passport (no check digit right, line 1 not starting with P).
- **Frames:** each camera frame is scaled to 256×256 on the GPU (`createImageBitmap`) and handed to a background thread as is — no full-size copy in the page.
- **Following between the model's answers** (`scan-lab/track.js`): on every camera frame, up to 16 textured points inside the document are followed on a 320 px gray copy (pyramidal Lucas–Kanade, checked forward and back) and their affine motion moves the frame; a model answer that comes back late is carried forward by the motion since its frame. So the frame keeps up at the camera's rate (~30 frames/s, ~14 ms per frame) while the model runs at ~13; a real movement also restarts the autocapture countdown — the document gone 3 % away since the frame stuck, or 2 % in one camera frame; a tremble of the hand only carries the stuck frame along (owner, 2026-10-24).
- **The frame on screen:** a thin line, no corner dots; red while searching, green once it has sat on the document's edges for 0.25 s; once green it **sticks** (owner, 2026-10-24: it kept moving and the countdown kept starting again): a model answer within 4 % of it leaves it where it is, and a weak or missed answer (a blur, a glare) is outlived for 0.6 s — the autocapture does not fire on such a held answer; it glides toward the latest result on every screen frame. **Autocapture** when the green frame has held still for 1 s (a countdown shown); a Capture button too.
- **The shot** is shown at once, straightened by the live frame; the accurate model then refines the corners in the background and replaces the shot if they moved.
- **Upright:** the straightened shot is turned upright by the face on the document — a small face finder (YuNet, MIT, 0.2 MB, run at 320×320 — `scripts/scan-lab/yunet_dyn.py` frees its fixed 640 input) is run on all 4 quarter-turns (a first-turn shortcut was tried and dropped: the finder places the eyes above the mouth even on an upside-down face, so only comparing the 4 turns is right) and the turn with a face whose eyes are above its mouth wins (40 of 40 turned right on the owner's photos). A document with no face (the ספח) keeps its turn on the phone and is set upright on the server with its reading. A text-direction model (RapidOrientation) was tried and dropped: it takes upright Hebrew documents for upside down.

### S6 — the models learn from the corrections (after S1–S2; owner, 2026-10-18)

How it works now, end to end: **docs/scan-training.md**.

**Status:** built — collecting (the corner editor in the scan window, `scan_feedback`, 12-month deletion, the super-admin's bell notice; docs/scan.md → Training examples) and the training on the PC (`scripts/scan-train/`: `pull.py` → `train.py` → `exam.py` → `publish.py` / `clean.py`, run by the skill `/scan-train`, `.claude/skills/scan-train/SKILL.md`). The ONNX models are fine-tuned directly: `onnx2torch` turns them into PyTorch (checked to answer the same, ~10⁻⁶) and they are exported back (checked again); targets are Gaussian corner spots of the models' own spread (σ ≈ 2.4 heatmap px), the loss weighs the spots over the empty background, batch norms keep their statistics, mirrored pages and light changes are added. Run end to end in the cloud on 19 made-up examples (CPU); the first real run is on the owner's PC. The page kinds are collected too (Haiku's kind, the user's, a 256×256 copy of the page); **left:** the document type model — S7. The monthly reminder is the bell itself (the server counts); a push in the Claude app would need a scheduled task that can read the counts, which it cannot.

- **What is learned from:** every correction on the review screen — corners moved, a page's kind changed — is a training example.
- **Where the examples are kept and how they are written** (owner, 2026-10-18):
  - **automatically, in the background**: when the scan is finished as usual ("Done" — no extra step for anyone; a scan closed before that sends nothing, so only final answers count) the phone makes, for every corrected page, a 256×256 copy of the page (never the photo) and sends them with both answers in one request to a new function `scan_feedback` (signed-in users only); with no network the examples wait in a queue on the phone and go when it is back. It checks the login and the firm, then writes the copy to Firebase Storage `training/scan/<YYYY-MM>/<id>.jpg`, its record to Firestore `scanTraining/<id>` — `{doctypeModel, doctypeFixed, edgesModel, edgesFixed, moved, model, tenantId, createdAt, deleteAt}` — and updates the counters in `scanTrainingStats` (new examples since the last training, the average move). A page the model got right counts in the statistics only, with no copy.
  - no client reads or writes any of it (Storage and Firestore rules closed); only `scan_feedback` writes, and only `/scan-train` on the owner's PC, under the owner's Google account, downloads.
  - **kept 12 months**, then deleted by a Storage lifecycle rule on `training/` and a Firestore TTL policy on `deleteAt`.
  - **every firm's corrections are used**, with no switch (owner, 2026-10-18): the training data is the firms' own, kept private as above.
- **What is trained:** the light and the accurate DocAligner models (Apache-2.0: the fine-tuned weights are the firm's own); later a small document type model (ID / ספח / passport / contract / other) that replaces Haiku's kind. Not trained: YuNet (already right), the MRZ reader (check digits), Haiku / Sonnet (Anthropic's — only their prompts and examples improve).
- **Where:** on the owner's PC (RTX 4070, 12 GB: ~15–30 min for both corner models on ~500 examples), so the examples never leave the firm's hands; the copies are deleted from the PC after the run.
- **How:** a repo skill, `/scan-train` (`.claude/skills/scan-train/`), run in Claude Code on the PC: download the new examples → train → an exam against a fixed set of photos with known answers (the old model against the new) → a "before → after" report → on the owner's yes, the new model goes to Hugging Face as the version `v<N>` (docs/hf-plan.md). **The lab learns first, the site follows** (owner, 2026-10-18): each copy names the version it uses — `scripts/scan-lab/models-version` and `scripts/scan/models-version` (both `base-v1` now; read by its `fetch-models.sh` at deploy); a new version is first set for the lab and tried on the phone, and only when it is better is it set for the site (one line, a deploy); the phones take it by its ETag. The old versions stay for a rollback. The training code is in `scripts/scan-train/`.
- **The agent:** a scheduled task, once a month, counts the new examples and runs the exam on the current model; when ~200 new examples are in or the score drops, it tells the owner to run `/scan-train` — in the app's bell (the super-admin's, until the retraining is done) and as a push in the Claude app; a month with nothing to do sends nothing. It never trains nor swaps a model itself.
- **The owner's side** (approved 2026-10-18): the notice "time to retrain" comes → on the PC, Claude Code, `/scan-train` → about an hour later the report → yes or no.

### S7 — the document type model (owner, 2026-10-19)

Replaces Haiku's page kind and upright turn (`aiDetectAlign`) with the firm's own model on the phone (owner, 2026-10-24: only the phone's Lite0 is trained and published — no server code runs a scanner model since 2026-10-19).

**Status:** prepared (2026-10-19), waiting for the page copies. Every page whose kind was changed, and one right page in five (`rightSeen`), sends a 256×256 copy of the cut, upright page with the kind it was given and the user's (docs/scan-training.md → 1); `scanTrainingStats/current.doctypesSince` counts the copies of each kind (by the kind the user left). When every kind but "Other" has **20** (`feedback.DOCTYPE_AT`; owner, 2026-10-20), the super-admin's bell says **Enough pages for the document type model**; the owner then runs `/scan-train` (its page-kind steps). Everything else is in place and switched off until a model version holds the models:
- **Training** (`scripts/scan-train/`): `pull_doctypes.py` → `train_doctypes.py` (timm EfficientNet-Lite, two heads: kind and turn; the phone's model only, `--phone` sets its size) → `exam_doctypes.py` (against Haiku; prints the next size up when a model is worse) → `publish.py --doctypes` (puts `doctype-lite.onnx` into the next version — a `doctype.onnx` is never carried forward —, resets `doctypesSince`; leaves out a model the exam rejected unless `--force-doctypes`, refused while the only exam is "your documents" with a type or the turn not measured; docs/scan-training.md → 4). A server's Lite2 is trained only with `train_doctypes.py --only server`, and never published. Dry-run in the cloud on made-up pages (random start, `--scratch`): train, ONNX export and its check, exam, the server reading the exported model.
- **The models' contract:** input `img` [1, 3, 256, 256] RGB 0..1 (the page stretched to the square, as the copies are); outputs `kind` [1, 6] and `turn` [1, 4] — probabilities; sure = ≥ 0.9.

### S8 — every scan attempt is sent for training (owner, 2026-10-20)

**Status:** S8-1 built (the server: `scan_feedback` `attempt` / `attemptOutcome`, the folders, `attemptsSince`, the bell at 15 — docs/scan-training.md → 2, 3); S8-2 built (every attempt sent from the lab and the site, the deleted pages, the queue, the readouts — docs/scan.md → The scan window); S8-3 built (the corner editor in the lab's preview, opened from the scan window too); S8-4 built (`/scan-train` trains on the attempts — `scripts/scan-train/attempts.py`, the picture exam, the attempts trained on moved to `attempts/used/v<N>/` by the publish, those kept as the exam to `attempts/exam/` — docs/scan-training.md → 4). S8 is built; the first training runs on the owner's PC when the bell says 15.

Every attempt made by anyone who scans becomes a training example with no extra step (owner, 2026-10-20: everyone who works sends data for training). This covers every login of every firm in the site's scanner (`/scan/`, the scan window) and the super-admin in the lab (`/scan-lab/`). The models learn from real work on real phones.

- **The lab and the site are one scanner** (owner, 2026-10-20): the site's scanner (`scan/`) is the lab's page copied as it is, so the sending, the queue, the corner editor and the readouts are the same code in both. Only `source` (`lab` / `site`) tells them apart. Most attempts come from the site, because the firms' users scan there every day.
- **The order of a release** (unchanged):
  1. The lab tries the new code or model version (the super-admin, on the phone).
  2. The owner approves it.
  3. It is copied into `scan/` (code) or set in `scripts/scan/models-version` (models).
  4. On the next deploy the site's scanner is the same as the lab's again.

  The site never runs anything the lab has not run first.

- **What is an attempt:** every camera shot and every page of a picked photo or PDF. For each one the scanner keeps:
  - the picture, at most 1600 px on the long side, JPEG;
  - the models' corners, the model version (`models-version`) and the way it ran (GPU / pool / CPU);
  - the page kind when the version has a document type model;
  - the outcome: kept (Looks good), retaken, or a page deleted from the batch;
  - the corrected corners when the user moved them. The scanner's preview (the lab's page, so the site's too) gets a 4-corner editor: drag a corner, and the moved corners become the answer.
- **A retake is sent too:** a retake of the same document sends both attempts, linked by one `group` id.
  - The earlier attempt is marked `retaken`, the later one `kept`.
  - The pair shows the training what went wrong (the earlier attempt) and what was right (the later one).
- **Sent at once:** each attempt goes in the background right after it is made, as one JSON request to `scan_feedback` with a new action `attempt`. The request carries the user's ID token. The server checks only that it is a signed-in login of a firm (any role, as S6), so that no stranger can fill the bucket with junk or poisoned examples, and records the firm (`tenantId`) and where the attempt came from (`source`: `site` / `lab`).
  - Nothing is kept on the phone when the network is up.
- **No network:** the attempt waits in a small queue on the phone (IndexedDB) and goes with the next attempt or when the network is back.
  - At most **5** attempts (pages) wait; the oldest is dropped beyond that (owner, 2026-10-20).
  - An attempt waits at most **1 hour**, then it is dropped.
  - The scanner shows **In queue: N / 5** while anything waits.
- **Where it is kept:** only the private Hugging Face bucket `OutStaff/scanner-data` (never a public repo), with the server's write-only token, as the site's examples. The attempts have their own folders and are never mixed with S6's examples:
  - new attempts go to `attempts/new/<source>/<YYYY-MM-DD>/<id>/` (`picture.jpg` + `answer.json`), where `<source>` is `site` or `lab`;
  - after a training (with the edges published), `publish.py --edges` moves the ones trained on to `attempts/used/v<N>/` and the exam ones to `attempts/exam/` (pulled as the exam on every later run, never trained on), so the next run takes only the new ones; `attempts/golden/` is never moved.
- **When to train:** the server counts the attempts in `attempts/new/` (`scanTrainingStats/current.attemptsSince`, site and lab together).
  - At **15**, the super-admin's bell says **New scan attempts — time to train**. The lab also shows **New for training: N / 15** (the super-admin only).
  - `/scan-train` takes `attempts/new/` (`pull.py --attempts`) together with the site's corrections:
    - the target corners are the moved corners, or the model's own corners for a kept attempt;
    - a retaken attempt with no moved corners is used only as a hard example in the exam, never as a target;
    - **a deleted page teaches what not to find** (owner, 2026-10-20). A page the user deleted from a kept batch (its outcome `deleted`) is a frame the model found where there is no document to keep — the extra third page cut from a photo of two. Its frame is trained as "nothing here": the next document search (`findNext`) learns to leave such a spot alone, and the exam counts the extra pages found before → after.
  - **The exam shows pictures too** (owner, 2026-10-20). Besides the figures, `exam.py` writes `~/.scan-train/report.html` on the owner's PC and opens it in the browser:
    - the 10 exam pages where the old and new models differ most;
    - each page with the old model's corners, the new model's corners and the right ones drawn on it, with the corner error of each model.

    The owner sees at a glance where it got better and where worse. The page stays on the PC only, because it shows workers' documents; `clean.py` / `publish.py` delete it with the copies.
  - Then the exam, and on the owner's yes the publish, which moves the attempts (to `attempts/used/` and `attempts/exam/`) and lowers `attemptsSince` by the folders really moved.
  - As always, the new version is set for the lab first and for the site only when it is better.
