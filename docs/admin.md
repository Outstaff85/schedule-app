# Admin tab

The Admin tab (`#sectionAdmin`) and the punch-log data behind it: the punch log, firms (every firm for the super-admin, the own firm for an owner), users, errors and timings. The whole tab is React, inside the old page: the section tabs, the tiles and the Punch Log (stage 6f-1: `src/manager/Admin.tsx` with its figures in `src/manager/punchlog.ts`), Firms (`src/manager/Firms.tsx`), Users (`src/manager/Users.tsx` with its rules in `src/manager/users.ts`), Errors and Timings (`src/manager/Logs.tsx` with its figures in `src/manager/adminlogs.ts`) (stage 6f-2), all drawn into the root `#adminRoot` through `ManagerIsland.renderAdmin()`; the super-admin's landing screen's firms into `#firmsScreenRoot` (`renderFirmsScreen()`); the firm, user and Add workers windows into the page's overlays `#editFirmOverlay` / `#userFormOverlay` / `#hrAssignOverlay` (`openFirm()`, `openUser()`, `openAssign()`). The data, its caches, the saves of workers and the server calls stay on the page and go through `ManagerBridge.admin()` and its actions ([build.md](build.md) → The manager's React pieces).

## Panes and who sees them

`switchTab('admin')` → `setupAdminPaneBar()` (the users prefetch for an owner / super-admin) → `switchAdminPane(null)`. The tab opens with **no section**: under the tabs "Choose a section" and **large tiles** — Punch Log, Firms, Users, Contract templates (those the login sees; a plate each with its icon and what the section is for; the Firms tile amber while the firm's Form 101 profile is incomplete) — plus, for a super-admin, **Scanner** (Admin → Scanner, below) and **Scanner lab**, which opens `/scan-lab/` in a new tab ([scan-plan.md](scan-plan.md) → Scanner lab) — and nothing is loaded until a tab or a tile is tapped (`ManagerBridge.setAdminPane` → `switchAdminPane(pane)`, kept in `__adminPane` while the tab stays open; every entry to the tab — Admin in the menu or the bottom bar's More, tapped again too — starts at the tiles; a section the login doesn't see is no section). `switchAdminPane` keeps the section, starts the load of Errors / Timings and draws the tab (`renderAdmin()`, which `render()` and `switchTab` call too; Firms and Users load their own data when they are drawn).

| Pane | Who |
|---|---|
| Punch Log | a staff login needs its Punch log tick (`punchlog`); the owner and the super-admin always; an HR never (`HR_HIDDEN_TABS`); a supplier or client works in the Portal and never reaches it (`tabAllowed`, `src/manager/logic/access.ts`) |
| Firms, Users | `superadmin`, `tenant_admin` |
| Contract templates | `tenant_admin`, and a `superadmin` inside a firm (`viewingTenantId`) — [contracts.md](contracts.md) → Contract templates |
| Errors, Timings, Scanner | `superadmin` |

The list is `adminPanes()` on the page (the React tabs draw only these). An `hr` login never sees the Admin tab ([hr.md](hr.md)).

Layout: the app's template (variant 3, [app-core.md](app-core.md) → Layout) in every section — figures on top, sections with a count, a `Plate` per item with a coloured bar on the left. The section tabs are the glass tabs (`.og-tabs`, icon + name; they scroll sideways on a narrow phone), the open one lit, and under them the open section's line (what it is for). Admin has no list / cards toggle (no tables left).

The punch log's Log / By Client switch is a segmented switch (`ToggleGroup`); every list is plates. A punch plate has a bare ⋮ (Edit, Delete) and a swipe on a phone; Delete never asks: the plate goes behind the React Undo snack and `ManagerBridge.deletePunches(ids)` (the page's `deletePunchesNow`: the cache, then `gsDeletePunch` each) runs when the 5 s are up; Select (a button above the log, the boxes after it) deletes the ticked punches the same way; until then the ids are kept in the tab (`hidden` in `Admin.tsx`) and the Log and By Client views leave them out, so a redraw during the Undo time doesn't bring them back. **Export CSV** and **Clear Log** are icon buttons at the right of the Log / By Client line; Clear Log is only for `tenant_admin` and `superadmin` (`canClear`). Opening the tab as an owner also preloads the user list (`prefetchAdminUsers()`). The section lines are `SUBTITLES` in `Admin.tsx`.

## Punch log data

- **Storage:** punches live in `localStorage['punchLog:' + firm]` — one key per firm (`fsTenantId()`), so a super-admin who opens another firm never sees the last one's punches; the punch page keeps its own `punchLog` (`src/punch/core.ts`). `getPunchLog()` parses it on every call, `savePunchLog(log)` writes it; when `localStorage` is full the list is kept in memory instead (`getPunchLog` returns that copy), so punches never disappear and the `punches` event still fires. Sign-out removes these keys ([app-core.md](app-core.md) → Login and session).
- **Loading:** `syncPunchesFromGS()` → `gsLoadPunchLog()` → `mergeRemotePunches(remote)`:
  - `time` is rebuilt from `ts` in local time;
  - remote rows win by id;
  - local rows the server doesn't have are kept if they are newer than `__punchLoadedFrom`.
- **Loading by month:**
  - `gsLoadPunchLog()` starts a live listener (`listenPunches`) from `punchWindowStart()` = the 1st of this month minus 7 days; every later snapshot is merged and refreshes Today;
  - `__punchLoadedFrom` is the earliest time loaded in this app open;
  - `ensurePunchesSince(from)` reads older punches once (`punchesBetween(t, from, loadedFrom)`), merges them and lowers `__punchLoadedFrom` (put back if the merge fails); it resolves `false` (after a toast) when they could not be loaded. Calls run one after another (a promise chain kept inside `src/manager/data/punches.ts`, where this state lives; `__punchLoadedFrom` is the page's name for `punchLoadedFrom()`). `ensureAllPunches()` = from 2000-01-01;
  - `periodPunchStart(period)` gives the start a salary period needs (a past month starts one day early);
  - callers: the Accounting period, salary and hours exports, Export CSV, Clear Log, an approved hours batch, and "Load earlier month" / "Load all history" under the log (`ManagerBridge.loadEarlierPunches` / `loadAllPunches`);
  - cached rows older than what this app open loaded are dropped on merge, so the cache doesn't grow;
  - another firm's punches (super-admin summary) use the same window (`punchesOnce`).
- **Writing:**
  - `gsSavePunch(entry)` / `gsUpdatePunch(data)` / `gsDeletePunch(id)` write to Firestore directly;
  - `updatePunch` writes only `type`, `time`, `site`, `notes`, `clientId`, `ts` (`savePunchEdit` moves it to the edited time — `editedPunchTs`, `src/manager/logic/punches.ts`: on the punch's own local day, or the day before / after when that keeps its shift whole — an OUT after the worker's IN before it and within 24h of it, an IN before the OUT after it and within 24h of it, the nearest to the old stamp; a punch with no such neighbour keeps its day; the live sync rebuilds `time` from `ts`), `date` / `day` when `date` is a DD/MM/YYYY (they follow a time moved to another day) and `editedAt`;
  - display helpers: `punchSite(e)` (the client from `clientId`, else the stored site, else the worker's first client), `latestOf(entries)`.

## Punch Log pane

Drawn by `PunchLog` in `src/manager/Admin.tsx` from `ManagerBridge.admin()`: `log` (the cached punches as stored, each with its site worked out by `punchSite` and its stored site text as `rawSite`; only while the section is open), the workers (id, name, clients), the clients (id, name, colour, address), `loadedFrom` and `canClear`. The figures are `src/manager/punchlog.ts`.

- **Stats:** Total IN, Total OUT, Today and Total (over what is loaded) — glass tiles (`Figures`, `logFigures`).
- **Views:** Log or By Client (a `ToggleGroup`), with the record count (Log) and the Export CSV / Clear Log icons on the same line; the view and the filters stay while the app stays open.
- **Filters** (four fields — one row on a computer, two columns on a phone; the caption is the field's label):
  - Worker — the workers that appear in the log;
  - Client — the punch's own `clientId` (where it was recorded); a punch saved without one falls back to the worker's current clients;
  - Date — the one Date Picker (`DatePicker format="dmy"`, docs/components.md → `date-picker.tsx`): only the days that have punches can be picked (`days` = `filterChoices`' dates, `en-GB` strings), its calendar opens at the newest of them, and its "All dates" line empties the filter;
  - Type.

  `filterChoices` / `filterLog`; the list is sorted newest first by `ts`.
- **Plates** (a `Plate` per punch, `PagedList` in up to three columns): green for IN and red for OUT on the left; the worker's name, under it IN/OUT · date · site · GPS (distance, ±accuracy, ⚠ with the warnings as its title when suspicious, or "Manual"); the time at the right; ⋮ and a swipe.
  - **Drawing:** 10 at first, the next 10 when the user scrolls the "Show more" row into view, or taps it ([app-core.md](app-core.md) → Long lists). A redraw keeps the opened count; a new filter starts again at 10 (the list is keyed by the filters).
  - **Older punches:** under the list, while not everything is loaded, "Showing punches from <date>" with **Load earlier month** (`loadEarlierPunchMonth()` loads back to the 1st of the month already partly loaded, or the month before) and **Load all history** (`ensureAllPunches()`).
- **Edit:** a tap, a double click or a long press on a punch plate (in the log or By Client; with a mouse a single click waits 250 ms for a double one, `useTapOpen`), or ⋮ → Edit, opens the punch window (`Window`): type, time, site (a client, "— No site —", or a kept custom site) and notes; **Save Changes** → `ManagerBridge.savePunch(id, edit)` → the page's `savePunchEdit`: a client sets `clientId` and its name as `site`, no site clears `clientId` and puts the worker's client name, the custom site is kept as it was; `editedBy:'admin'` and `editedAt`; then `gsUpdatePunch`, `render()` and the toast.
- **By Client** (`byClient`): per client a panel (its colour dot, name, address; "N on site now · N workers · N total punches"), a plate per assigned worker (green while on site — today's latest punch is IN; "In since …" / "Last: … (IN/OUT)" / "No records today"; "N records" at the right; a tap opens its card) and its last 5 punches, newest first, as small plates under it (the time as the name; a tap edits).
- **Export CSV** (`ManagerBridge.exportPunches()` → `ensureAllPunches()` then, only when the whole history came in, the page's `exportPunchCSV()` — never a file with part of it): Worker, Supplier, Type, Time, Date, Site, Distance(m), GPS Accuracy (rounded), Notes, Edited — written by `csvText` (`src/lib/utils.ts`: a UTF-8 BOM so Excel shows Hebrew names, and a text cell starting with = + - @ gets a leading apostrophe, never run as a formula).
- **Clear Log** (owner / super-admin; after `ensureAllPunches()` — it stops there when the whole history could not be loaded, `loadAllPunches()` resolving `false`), **behind the Alert Dialog** (it asks, as an irreversible action; it does not close on the backdrop):
  1. A month checklist with counts per month (`monthCounts` / `punchMonthKey`: from `date`, else `ts`) and Select all; the user must type `DELETE`; Delete (with the count) is off until a month is ticked and the word typed; Cancel keeps everything.
  2. `ManagerBridge.clearPunchMonths(months)` → the page's `clearPunchMonths`: downloads a CSV of exactly those rows first,
  3. then `deletePunchesByMonths` runs (by `day`, or `ts` for a punch without `day`), the rows are dropped from the local cache, `render()`, and the toast gives the count. A failure keeps the dialog open with its toast.

## Firms pane

Drawn by `FirmsPane` in `src/manager/Firms.tsx` from `ManagerBridge.admin()`: `isSuper`, `firms` (a super-admin's list — the cached one, `localStorage.firmsCache_v1`, until `listTenants` answers — each row with `missing`, what Form 101 would leave empty, `firmProfileMissing(t)`; null until something is there, with `firmsError`), `own` (an owner's own profile with its `missing`) and `locale`. Drawing the section (and the landing screen) calls `ManagerBridge.loadFirms()` → the page's `loadFirms()`: for a super-admin `listTenants` (saved in the cache and `__firmsCache`), for an owner `refreshTenantProfile(0)`; then both views draw again (`renderFirmsViews`). Skeleton plates while nothing is loaded.

**Firm owner** (`tenant_admin`): the own firm only (`OwnFirm`): one large plate (green when Form 101 has everything, amber when not) with the Form 101 fields (name, deductions file, phone, address) and which of them are missing; a tap, a double click or a long press opens the firm window for the firm being worked in (`openFirmWindow('mine')` → the page's `openMyFirmForm`). There is no super-admin bar and no separate Profile pane.

**Super-admin:**

- **List** (`FirmsList`, the same component on the landing screen `#firmsScreen` — its header (Firms, the Language button, Create firm, Sign out) is React too, `FirmsScreen`, stage 8e-2 — and in Admin → Firms):
  - a plate per firm (`PagedList`, one column — a firm is never a narrow third of a computer screen; owner, 2026-10-27): green while active, amber when disabled or its profile is incomplete; the name, then worker and client counts and the last save (left out when the date can't be read), then "disabled" / "profile incomplete" (its title lists the missing fields);
  - a tap → `openFirm(id)` → `switchToTenant()` (with a mouse a single click waits 250 ms for a double one, `useTapOpen`); a double click, a long press or the ✎ at its right → the firm window (`openFirmWindow('edit', id)` → `openEditFirmForm`); ⋮: Edit, Disable / Enable (`setFirmsActive` → the page's `setTenantsActive`: `setTenantActive` each, its toast, the list again), Delete; a swipe left on a phone (Edit, Delete);
  - **Delete never asks**: the plate goes behind the React Undo snack (`hiddenFirms` keeps it out of a redraw) and `deleteFirms(ids)` (the page's `deleteTenantsNow`: `deleteTenant` each — the firm's data, its logins and every worker file in Storage — then the toast and the list) runs when the 5 s are up;
  - **Select** (a button above the list): Disable / Enable / Delete the ticked firms.
- **Admin → Firms only:** above the list **Payable summary (all firms)** (a toggle, kept open while the app is) and **New firm**. The summary (`firmsPayable()` → the page's `firmsPayableRows()`, [accounting.md](accounting.md) → Super-admin summary) shows a plate per firm with this week's hours and pay, "(load failed)" in red for a firm that could not be read, and the TOTAL on the section's line.
- **Firm window** (`FirmWindow`, drawn into `#firmModal` of `#editFirmOverlay`; what it edits is the page's `__firmWin` from `showFirmWindow(mode, tenantId, profile)`, read with `firmInfo()`):
  - fields: company name, deductions file number, phone, street, street number, city — their labels Hebrew, as Form 101 prints them; the note under the title Hebrew too;
  - **Create** (`openCreateFirmForm`) also asks for the owner's name, username and password → `createUser` (`adminCall`); a missing username or password is an error in the window;
  - **Edit** (`openEditFirmForm`, a firm of the list's cache) or the firm's own profile (`openMyFirmForm`, the session's copy at once, then `loadProfile()` — `loadTenantProfile` — fills the form unless something was already typed) → `updateTenant`;
  - after saving (`firmSaved` on the page): the window closes, the toast, `cacheTenantProfile`, the list again (super-admin), and `regenerateAllWorkerDocs()` when the edited firm is the one loaded (every worker's Form 101 employer block changes). For another firm, the rebuild waits until that firm is opened here (`form101Regen:<tenantId>`, `maybeRegenAfterFirmEdit`);
  - **Signature and stamp** (Edit and the firm's own profile, not Create): the firm's signature and stamp (`FirmSealSection`, `src/manager/FirmSeal.tsx` — [contracts.md](contracts.md) → The firm's signature and stamp), saved on their own, not part of `updateTenant`; a super-admin edits the firm it opened (`asTenantId`);

Empty, or the default text, = the default `waDefaultTemplate()` in the UI language: "Hi {full name}, you have not punched in today (shift {start} · {site})." Placeholders: `{full name}`, `{first name}`, `{start}` (today's planned start), `{site}` (the worker's first client, `workerSiteName`).
  - The button: a no-punch notice (notices sheet and bell panel) of a worker with a phone gets **WhatsApp** next to **Call** (`noPunchActs`). ## Users pane (owner, super-admin)

- **Permission templates** (owner, 2026-10-19: "the template is always global"): a login's **role and rights** come from a template, and the templates are **one set for every firm** — only a super-admin creates, edits and deletes them; a firm's owner puts each login on one and reads them. The rights are **one flat list** (the plan it came from, done: [archive/docs/permissions-plan.md](../archive/docs/permissions-plan.md); `RIGHTS` in `functions-auth/core.py`, mirrored in `src/manager/templates.ts`): a tick is one thing and depends on no other — **Tabs** Today, Schedule, Workers, Clients, Suppliers, Accounting, Punch log, Commission, Hours; **Actions** Edit shifts, Edit workers, Edit / delete punches, Edit pay, Export, Approve hours, Dispute hours, Fix hours, Set the signing date (`setSignDate`), Firm's signature and stamp; client contracts (`firmSign`: uploads, replaces and deletes the firm's signature and stamp — [contracts.md](contracts.md) → The firm's signature and stamp; staff and HR only); **Shown** See workers' pay; and **Whose workers** (an HR: Only own workers or the whole firm; a supplier / client: always its own; staff: always the firm's). Each role's template may tick only its own ticks (`RIGHT_CAPS`): a supplier or client never gets Clients, Accounting or the Punch log (a client no money tick at all), an HR not the firm-wide Today, Punch log or the full Accounting (it gets Commission), staff not the review ticks; the server drops any other. Kept in `permTemplates/{id}` (function-only, `firestore.rules`): `name`, `role` (staff / hr / supplier / client), `rights` (the flat list), `ownOnly` (HR) and `updatedAt`. The rights are **copied** into each login's `users` doc with its `templateId`; `saveTemplate` copies them again into every login on it in every firm and ends the sessions of those whose rights changed ("Template saved · N logins sign in again"), so a tick added or removed reaches everyone. A template's role is fixed while logins use it. Deleting one (Undo snack) leaves its logins' rights as they are, without a template. The first `listTemplates` writes the defaults, once (`appMeta/templates` remembers it: deleting them all leaves no templates, they don't come back): Full access (staff: every staff tick but Set the signing date and Firm's signature and stamp, which are off by default), Dispatcher (Today, Schedule, Workers, Punch log, the three edits), Accountant (Today, Workers, Accounting, Punch log, the edits, Edit pay, Export), HR (Schedule, Workers, Hours, the edits, the three review ticks, See workers' pay, Only own workers), Supplier (Today, Workers, Commission, Hours, the review ticks, See workers' pay), Client (Hours and the review ticks). Server: `list_templates` / `save_template` / `delete_template` and `_load_template` in `functions-auth/core.py` ([backend.md](backend.md)).
  - **Templates section** (`TemplatesSection` in `src/manager/Users.tsx`, above Logins; the list in `src/manager/templates.ts` — `loadTemplates` / `useTemplates`, read once and shared with the user window): a plate per template in its role's colour, a summary of what it gives (`templateSummary`: "Tabs 3 · Actions 6 · only own workers · sees workers' pay") and how many logins use it (every firm for a super-admin, the own firm for an owner); a tap opens the **template window** (`TemplateWindow`, the shared `Window`): Template name, For role (locked while logins use it; a new role drops the ticks it can't have), the flat ticks as tiles in three groups (`RightsTiles`: Tabs, Actions, Shown — a tick the role can never have greyed with the reason, `capReason`) and Whose workers (Only own workers: an HR's choice, locked on for a supplier and a client, off for staff), and Save — for a super-admin; an owner sees it all read-only, with no Save. A super-admin also has **New template** and Delete under ⋮ / a swipe.
  - **Who gets what** (`RightsTable`, folded under the templates; `rightsMatrix` in `templates.ts`): every tick down the side — Tabs, Actions, Shown, Whose workers — the owner (everything) and each template side by side: ✓, —, or blank where the role can never have it.
  - **The edit ticks in the app** (`canChange` in `src/manager/logic/access.ts`, from `session.rights` — the login reply, refreshed from the ID token's `rt` claim at start): without Edit shifts the Schedule tab only reads (`canEdit`); without Edit workers the Workers tab, Today's Add and the worker card only read; without Edit punches a punch in the log doesn't open its edit window, has no Delete or Select, and Manual punch is gone from the Clients tab and the client window. The rules enforce the same ([backend.md](backend.md) → Security rules). A staff login also has Today and the Punch log as their own ticks — without Today it opens on its first tab, without Punch log the Admin tab is gone (`tabAllowed` / `homeTab`) — and changes a worker's pay only with Edit pay (`staffPayEdit`; the rules: `editPay`).
  - A login made before templates keeps its rights and shows "No template" on its plate until it is put on one. A super-admin sees how many such logins all firms have and **Put them on templates** (`linkToTemplates`, `link_to_templates` in `core.py`; owner, 2026-10-19): each goes on the template of its role giving exactly its rights (its rights read as a template's, `_template_rights`), else the one sharing the most rights (the fewest extra on a tie); its rights become the template's, and those whose rights changed sign in again (the toast gives both counts). Owners and the super-admin are left alone.
- **List** (`UsersPane` in `src/manager/Users.tsx`): drawing it calls `loadUsers()` → the page's `prefetchAdminUsers()` (`listUsers` through `fetchAdminUsers`, which a load in flight shares; the logins of the active firm only, `activeTenantIdForAdmin()` — `listUsers` returns more for a super-admin; cached as `adminCache_users_<tenantId>`; the HR names on the worker plates, Payable by HR and an open worker window draw again). The snapshot's `users` is that cache (null until it exists, with `usersError`), `team` the workers with their `hrId` / `supplierId`, `suppliers` and `clients` their names.
  - the firm's owner (`tenant_admin`, a pin) is always first, then a super-admin, then everyone by name (`sortUsers`);
  - a "Logins" section with the count and **New user**, then a plate per login (`PagedList columns`), its role as the coloured bar (`ROLE_COLOR` in `users.ts`: owner amber, super-admin accent, HR violet `--uline-1`, supplier cyan `--uline-2`, client green, staff grey): the person's name (`fullName`, the login when it has none) with the role and an inactive flag; then `login: <username> · <phone>`; then its template ("No template" in amber when it has none); then the supplier (supplier role), the client (client role) or its tabs (staff, hr: the flat rights' tabs), with an HR's worker count or a supplier's count of its workers ("sees all workers" for an HR with Only own workers off); then the hours ticks (Hours, Approve / Dispute / Fix hours, from its flat rights);
  - a tap, a double click or a long press edits (`openUserWindow(id)`); ⋮ (Edit, Delete) and a swipe left on a phone (Edit, Delete). **Delete never asks**: the plate goes behind the Undo snack and `deleteUsers(ids)` (the page's `deleteUsersNow`: a deleted HR's workers are unassigned first — `assignWorkersToHr(ids, null)`, which also clears its hrId from their punches, days off and shift offers — then `deleteUser` each, the toast, the list again) runs only when its 5 s are up. **Select** (the Select button at the right of the Logins line, after New user): every login but the owner's and a super-admin's (`UNSELECTABLE`) gets a tick box; the glass bar's Delete removes the picked logins the same way (one Undo for all).
- **User window** (`UserWindow`, drawn into `#userFormModal` of `#userFormOverlay`; `openCreateUserForm(preset)` / `openEditUserForm(id)` on the page — a saved login from the list's cache, the server only when the row isn't there): a head (`EditorHead`: the initials in the role's colour, the full name live, "HR · 14 own workers" / the role and its supplier or client, and for a saved login that is not the owner's a ⋮ with Delete — the window closes and the plate goes behind the Undo snack), then section plates (`Accordion`; Person & login open each time) in the owner's fixed order, and one Save:
  - **Person & login**: Full name ("Full name *" for HR, where it is required), Phone, Username (locked for a saved login), Password ("Password (min. 8)" for a new login; "New password (empty = keep)" when editing — filled in, it is sent as `resetUserPassword` after the other fields are saved (`updateUser`), since it ends the user's sessions; one's own password changed, the window closes with "Password changed — please sign in again" and the app signs out on purpose). There is no role switch: the template says the role;
  - **Rights** (`data-sec="sections"`): **Rights come from** — the template select (every template, "Name · Role"; a login made before templates: "No template (rights set before templates)" until one is picked); picking one gives the login its role (`withTemplate` in `users.ts`: a supplier the first supplier, a client the first client, kept when already picked); for a supplier the supplier select, for a client the client select (whose workers are "own" — the login's, not the template's); the lock line "Role and rights follow <template>; only the super-admin changes the template"; then the template's ticks, **read-only** (`RightsTiles`, as in the template window), for a client the note that it never sees pay, rates, suppliers, GPS or documents;
  - **HR commission** (HR): "HR commission ₪/h" (`commissionRate`) and "₪/day (daily workers)" (`commissionDayRate`, empty = by the hour); its summary is the rate and this month's commission on its workers (`hrMonthCommission` → the Salary rows and `commissionFor`, as in Payable by HR);
  - **Own workers** (an HR's saved login, and a supplier's): the team's workers (paged, each with Unassign) and **Add workers**. The team is an HR's workers (`hrId` = its uid; `assignWorkers('hr', …)` → `assignWorkersToHr`, which also moves their punches) or the workers of the supplier picked in the form (`supplierId`; `assignWorkers('supplier', …)` → `assignWorkersToSupplier`: sets `supplierId`, saves — punches carry no supplier), so every login of that supplier shares them; the section shows for a supplier also while creating, and follows the supplier select.
  - **Add workers** (`AssignWindow`, `#hrAssignModal` of `#hrAssignOverlay`, opened over the user window): the firm's other workers with a search (name or document) and a tick tile each, a worker of another HR or supplier marked "now: <name>"; **Assign** saves them, the toast gives the count.
  - A new login starts on the first template of its role (Staff, or the supplier / client it was opened for) once the list is in.
  - An owner's (or a super-admin's) row shows only Person & login; it saves only Full name and Phone (and the password).

## Errors and Timings: the period picker

Both sections (`LogsPane` in `src/manager/Logs.tsx`) load one period at a time, picked in the one month picker (`MonthPicker`, docs/components.md → `date-picker.tsx`) at the left of the toolbar:
- "Last 30 days" (default) and "Last 7 days" are its named periods above the months, and each calendar month (this one and the 11 before it; the others can't be picked) is a month of its grid — the page's `logPeriodOptions` (`m:YYYY-MM`);
- `logPeriodRange(value)` gives `{key, from, to, label}` (ISO times), sent to `loadErrors` / `loadTimings` as `from` / `to` (`logPeriodQuery`) by `loadLogPane(kind)`, which `switchAdminPane`, `setLogPeriod(kind, value)` and Refresh (`reloadLog`) call; the picked period is kept on the page (`__logPeriod`) while the app stays open;
- each period has its own cache (`adminCache_errors:<key>`, `adminCache_timings:<key>`): the snapshot's `logs` is the cached rows (null until loaded, with the load error), drawn at once and again when the fresh rows arrive;
- **Copy all** and **Print** take only the loaded period. Print opens a plain page with a table (for timings, the summary first) and prints it.

The toolbar is the select and icon buttons in one line: Copy all, Print, Refresh and **Reset** (a red bin at the right end). Times are shown as `04.10 10:18` (`shortWhen`). The lists page by 10. Copying goes through the page's `copyTextToClipboard` (`copyText`, with its toast).
